# SOAM Patent Watch

**Baseline documentation date:** 2026-09-06
**Status:** Research-control structure only

## Purpose

This file identifies technical areas that require prior-art and patent review as
SOAM develops.

PR-1 does not identify patent families, assignees, claim scope, infringement
positions, patentability conclusions, or freedom-to-operate conclusions.

Specific patent records may be added only after a sourced review.

## Areas to monitor

- decentralized adaptive graph control;
- invariant-guided distributed control;
- local state and health evaluation;
- bounded graph-edge or bridge-state adaptation;
- topology adaptation using persistence or hysteresis;
- graph resilience and spatial compatibility metrics;
- transport-independent distributed adaptation;
- mesh and MANET routing mechanisms adjacent to SOAM use cases;
- cooperative relay and RF coordination;
- multicast and forwarding-control mechanisms;
- timing and synchronization mechanisms;
- beamforming, MIMO, and baseband coordination;
- transmit-power and spectrum-control mechanisms.

## Review record template

For each future researched item record:

- source and publication identifier;
- publication date;
- priority date where verified;
- assignee/applicant where verified;
- relevant independent claims;
- potentially relevant dependent claims;
- SOAM component compared;
- prior-art relevance;
- preliminary FTO relevance;
- possible design-around considerations;
- reviewer/date;
- unresolved questions.

No claim-level conclusion should be entered without supporting sources and
documented analysis.
